Abstract:
Understanding the precise genetic -basis of disease is one of the critical developments in medicine in the twenty-first century. Genetic testing has revolutionized the diagnosis and treatment of neurological diseases in children. Whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing have particularly been useful in understanding the genetic basis of childhood epileptic encephalopathies characterized by early-onset seizures with significant developmental impairment and regression.
