Title: Genetic Advancements in Infantile Epileptic Spasms Syndrome and Opportunities for Precision Medicine.
Authors: Hannah E Snyder, Puneet Jain, Rajesh RamachandranNair, Kevin C Jones, Robyn Whitney
Journal: Genes
Year: 2024
MeSH Terms: Animals, Precision Medicine, Spasms, Infantile, Epilepsy, Epileptic Syndromes, Spasm

Abstract:
Infantile epileptic spasms syndrome (IESS) is a devastating developmental epileptic encephalopathy (DEE) consisting of epileptic spasms, as well as one or both of developmental regression or stagnation and hypsarrhythmia on EEG. A myriad of aetiologies are associated with the development of IESS; broadly, 60% of cases are thought to be structural, metabolic or infectious in nature, with the remainder genetic or of unknown cause. Epilepsy genetics is a growing field, and over 28 copy number variants and 70 single gene pathogenic variants related to IESS have been discovered to date.
